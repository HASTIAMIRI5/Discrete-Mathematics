# Questions: Graph Theory

## Question 1
A graph has 5 vertices and 7 edges. What is the sum of the degrees of all vertices?

### Solution
By the Handshaking Lemma, the sum of degrees is 2|E| = 14.

## Question 2
Is the graph K_4 bipartite?

### Solution
No. K_4 is complete on 4 vertices, and a bipartite graph cannot contain an edge between two vertices in the same part.

## Question 3
A connected graph has 6 vertices and 5 edges. Is it a tree?

### Solution
A tree on 6 vertices must have 5 edges. Since the graph is connected and has exactly 5 edges, it is a tree.

## Question 4
How many edges does K_6 have?

### Solution
K_6 has C(6,2) = 15 edges.

## Question 5
Does a graph with all vertices of even degree always have an Eulerian circuit?

### Solution
Not necessarily if the graph is disconnected. The condition is: every vertex has even degree and the graph is connected (except isolated vertices).
