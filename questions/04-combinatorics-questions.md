# Questions: Combinatorics and Counting

## Question 1
How many ways can 5 books be arranged on a shelf?

### Solution
5! = 120 ways.

## Question 2
How many ways can 3 students be chosen from 8?

### Solution
C(8,3) = 8!/(3!5!) = 56

## Question 3
How many two-digit numbers can be formed from digits 1, 2, 3, 4 if repetition is allowed?

### Solution
First digit: 4 choices, second digit: 4 choices. Total = 4 × 4 = 16.

## Question 4
In a class of 25 students, 15 play soccer and 10 play basketball. If 5 play both, how many play at least one sport?

### Solution
|A ∪ B| = 15 + 10 - 5 = 20

## Question 5
How many ways can 4 identical red balls and 3 identical blue balls be arranged in a row?

### Solution
Total objects = 7, duplicates reduce arrangements:
7!/(4!3!) = 35
